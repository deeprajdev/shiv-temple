Baidyanath Dham 🛕

An interactive guide to Baidyanath Dham, Deoghar, Jharkhand, made to help visitors explore the temple, pilgrimage routes, local food, markets, and nearby places.

The site combines a simple travel guide with an interactive 3D map and a small animated puja experience.

What's inside?
Guide — How to reach Baidyanath Dham, darshan, queues, Kanwar Yatra, markets, food, and nearby places.
Map — An interactive 3D view of the Kanwar route and temple area.
Puja — A small stylised animation inspired by the atmosphere of a puja. It isn't meant to represent the actual temple rituals.
🍛 Food

While you're in Deoghar, you can explore some local and popular options like:

Deoghar Peda — probably the most famous sweet to take back home.
Litti Chokha — a popular regional meal.
Dhuska — a Jharkhandi rice-and-lentil snack.
Kachori, Samosa & Jalebi — easy street-food options around the busy market areas.
Chai — because every pilgrimage needs a chai break.

⚠️ A small note

The visuals and 3D map on this website are creative interpretations made for this project and are not exact or real representations of Baidyanath Temple or its premises.
So if something looks different from the actual temple, please don't be angry😅 it's meant to be an interactive visual experience, not a replica of the real place.

This is an independent project and is not an official website of Baidyanath Temple or the Deoghar administration.
